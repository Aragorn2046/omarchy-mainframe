# Code of conduct

Mainframe follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, welcoming and constructive. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not
tolerated in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

Problems can be reported privately to the maintainer through the repository's **Security** tab
(**Report a vulnerability**) or by opening a minimal issue asking to be contacted. Reports are handled confidentially.
The maintainer may remove content or block participants who break these rules.
